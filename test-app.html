HOW TO MAXIMIZE GAMEPLAY

🎯 1. CORE MISSION & 8-WAY TRACING

Search the glowing field to locate all Target Words listed on the right sidebar. Drag horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in any direction. Release when selected!

⭐ 2. STREAK 2X MULTIPLIER TILES

Look for special glowing tiles labeled 2X. Include that letter in any found word to instantly double your multiplier score and trigger golden coin showers!

⚡ 3. DYNAMIC SCRAMBLE HAZARDS

As you advance through the Saga Map, levels introduce hazards: 🛡️ Safe Zone (calm search), 🌀 Shift (scramble countdown), ⚡ Quake (rapid earth-shaking shifts), and 🌊 Flood (bad swipes cause un-cleared letters to cascade downward!).

🐷 4. PIGGY BANK VAULT & SECRET WORDS

Discover valid English bonus words not on the target list to automatically deposit +15 Coins into your Piggy Bank! When filled to threshold, smash it to claim the golden stash directly into your wallet!

🗺️ 5. SAGA TRAIL & 100-COIN MILESTONES

Conquer levels through Neon Alley, Cyber Grid, and beyond. Every completed puzzle awards up to 3 stars ⭐, and every 5 levels triggers a 100-Coin Milestone Bonus celebrating that You're Crushing It!

🛠️ 6. TACTICAL POWER-UP ARSENAL

🔍 Reveal: Pulses an unsolved word and freezes scramble for 8s.
🌪️ Tornado: Triggers an instant safe reshuffle of un-cleared tiles.
❄️ Freeze: Pauses the scramble shift countdown for 8s.
🏝️ Scene: Cycles through glowing Azure Atoll, Palm Shore, and Coral Reef themes.
